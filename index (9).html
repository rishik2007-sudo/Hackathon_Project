Frequently asked
Why not just search the policy documents?

Search returns the document that looks most like your question. It has no idea that one of them was replaced in 2026 and is now wrong.

How does it know which rule wins?

The more specific rule wins. A policy naming your exact region and team beats one that says GLOBAL and ALL. A named-vendor exception beats everything.

What happens to old policy versions?

Plain Python removes them before the model runs, using the effective date and the supersedes field. The model can never quote a dead policy, because it never sees one.

What if the question is missing information?

It asks. If the answer changes by region and you never said your region, it asks for the region instead of guessing.

What does the AI actually decide?

Only the judgement call — which surviving rule governs, and how to say it. Dates and versions are maths, so Python handles those and is right every time.
